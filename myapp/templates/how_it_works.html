How SkillSwap Campus Works

SkillSwap Campus helps students learn from each other by connecting people who want to teach their skills with students who want to learn them.

1

Create Your Profile

Register on SkillSwap Campus and complete your student profile with your department, year and bio.

2

Add Your Skills

Select the skills you can teach and the skills you want to learn from other students.

3

Connect & Learn

Explore students, send learning requests and start meaningful skill exchanges with your peers.

What You Can Do

🔎 Explore Skills

Search for skills available on campus and discover students who are willing to teach them.

🤝 Send Learning Requests

Send a request to a student who teaches a skill you want to learn.

🔔 Real-Time Notifications

Receive instant notifications when someone sends, accepts or rejects a learning request.

⭐ Reviews & Ratings

After completing an accepted skill exchange, students can review their learning experience.

Discover new skills and connect with students who can help you grow.
